Remember October 20, 2020: Six Years After #EndSARS

Published: 20 October 2026
Category: Nigeria News

Featured Image

October 20, 2020 remains one of the most significant dates in recent Nigerian history. Six years later, the #EndSARS movement continues to represent the demand for justice, accountability and meaningful police reform.

Six Years Later: Remembering the #EndSARS Movement

The #EndSARS movement grew out of years of complaints about police brutality, harassment, unlawful arrests and abuses attributed to officers of the Special Anti-Robbery Squad (SARS).

In October 2020, thousands of Nigerians, particularly young people, took to the streets and social media to demand an end to police brutality. Their demonstrations spread across several Nigerian cities and attracted international attention.

October 20, 2020: The Lekki Toll Gate Incident

On October 20, 2020, protesters gathered at the Lekki Toll Gate in Lagos. That evening, security personnel opened fire during an incident that became a defining moment in the history of the movement.

The circumstances surrounding the shooting, including the number of people injured or killed, became subjects of investigations, public debate and competing accounts.

Remembering the Protesters

For many Nigerians, the movement was about more than the disbandment of a police unit. It represented a demand for dignity, freedom, safety and a government that listens to its citizens.

The demonstrations also showed the importance of peaceful assembly, freedom of expression and the role of young people in demanding change.

Six Years Later: What Has Changed?

The Lagos State Judicial Panel of Inquiry investigated the Lekki Toll Gate incident and received testimonies and evidence concerning the events of October 20.

Human rights organisations, including Amnesty International, have continued to call for transparency, accountability and justice for victims of alleged abuses.

The Nigerian Army disputed some allegations about its conduct and the events of that night. The different accounts remain part of the public record and discussion.

Why the Movement Still Matters

The #EndSARS movement remains an important chapter in Nigeria's modern history. It brought national attention to concerns about police conduct and demonstrated the power of citizens organising around shared demands.

Remembering October 20 means remembering those affected, listening to their experiences and continuing to demand institutions that protect the rights and dignity of every Nigerian.
